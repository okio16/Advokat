Your employer does not pay, or you were injured at work? The law gives you effective means to obtain what you are owed.

Unpaid remuneration

Salary is the employer's main obligation. When it is not paid on time, the employee is entitled to it together with statutory default interest. The limitation period for employment claims is three years.

Type of claimWhen it is due
SalaryMonthly, by the date in the contract
Compensation for unused paid annual leaveOn termination of the contract
Redundancy compensationOn dismissal due to redundancy, closure or reduced workload
Retirement compensationOn termination after acquiring the right to a pension – 2 or 6 gross salaries depending on length of service with the employer
Compensation for failure to give noticeWhen the contract is terminated without observing the notice period

How unpaid sums are collected

  1. A written demand to the employer – with a payment deadline.
  2. A report to the Labour Inspectorate – it can issue a binding instruction and impose a sanction.
  3. An application for an enforcement order or a statement of claim to the district court – with no court fee for the employee.
  4. If the employer is insolvent – a claim to the Fund for Guaranteed Receivables of Employees.

Workplace accidents

A workplace accident is any sudden harm to health that occurs during and in connection with or because of the work performed, as well as on the usual route from home to work and back. The accident must be declared to the National Social Security Institute – by the employer within a short period, and if it fails to do so, the injured person may declare it themselves.

The employer is financially liable for damage from a workplace accident, regardless of whether it or another of its employees is at fault. Liability may be reduced only if the injured person contributed to the accident through gross negligence.

  • Compensation for pain and suffering
  • The difference between the social security benefit and the full salary
  • Medical and rehabilitation costs
  • Compensation for relatives in case of death

If the accident happened on the way by car, there may also be a claim against an insurer – see road accident compensation.

Frequently asked questions

How long do I have to claim unpaid wages?

Three years from the date on which they should have been paid.

Does the employer owe compensation if the accident was my fault?

Yes, unless you acted intentionally. With gross negligence the compensation may be reduced, but it does not disappear.

What if the employer does not declare the accident?

You can declare it yourself to the National Social Security Institute. Do it as soon as possible and keep the medical documents.
