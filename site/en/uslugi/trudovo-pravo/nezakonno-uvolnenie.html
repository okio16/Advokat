What to do immediately after dismissal

  1. Write down the date on which you received the order.
  2. Do not sign documents in which you “agree” to the termination.
  3. Collect the employment contract, annexes, orders and emails.
  4. Register with the labour office – this matters for compensation.
  5. Contact a lawyer before the deadline expires.

If, in addition to the dismissal, sums remain unpaid, see unpaid wages. I represent employees from Mladost, the city centre, Nadezhda, Lyulin, all of Sofia and the rest of Bulgaria.

What you can ask the court for

In case of unlawful dismissal under the Labour Code the employee may bring four claims, usually together:

  1. Declaration that the dismissal is unlawful and its annulment
  2. Reinstatement in the previous job
  3. Compensation for the time out of work due to the dismissal – up to 6 months' gross salary
  4. Correction of the ground for dismissal in the employment record book and other documents

The claim is filed within two months of the termination of employment before the district court. The case is heard under the expedited procedure for employment disputes.

The most common grounds for unlawfulness

  • The order is not reasoned or does not clearly state the ground
  • In a disciplinary dismissal the employee was not heard before the penalty
  • The deadlines for imposing a disciplinary penalty were missed
  • In a redundancy, the selection was not made or was merely formal
  • An employee with special protection was dismissed without prior permission – a mother of a child under 3, a person with reduced working capacity, a person with certain illnesses, an employee on leave
  • The notice period was not observed or compensation was not paid

The burden of proving that the dismissal was lawful lies with the employer. This is a significant advantage for the employee.

Civil servants

Civil servants appeal the act of dismissal under the Administrative Procedure Code before the administrative court within 14 days of notification. If the act is annulled, the civil servant is reinstated and entitled to compensation. The procedure is also described on the appeals against administrative acts page.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get compensation without returning to work?

Yes. The reinstatement claim is not mandatory. You can seek annulment of the dismissal and compensation without returning.

What if I have started a new job?

Compensation is due for the time without work or in lower-paid work, so a new job reduces the amount but does not remove the right to claim.

When does the two-month period start?

From the day the employment was terminated – usually from service of the order.
