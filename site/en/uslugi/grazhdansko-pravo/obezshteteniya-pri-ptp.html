Road Accident Compensation in Sofia

Injured in a road accident? The insurer owes fair compensation – not the first offer it makes.

Who pays the compensation

In an accident caused by an insured driver, compensation is paid by the insurer under the at-fault driver's compulsory third-party liability insurance. If the at-fault driver is uninsured or unknown, the Guarantee Fund is liable. If your car has comprehensive insurance, you can receive compensation for the damage to it from your own insurer.

SituationWho the claim is against
At-fault driver with third-party insuranceThe at-fault driver's insurer
At-fault driver uninsured or unknownThe Guarantee Fund
Car with comprehensive insuranceYour own insurer (for damage to the car)
Foreign vehicleThrough the insurer's representative in Bulgaria or the National Bureau

What can be claimed

  • Pecuniary damage – repair or market value of the car, medical and medicine costs, rehabilitation, lost income
  • Non-pecuniary damage – pain and suffering, stress, prolonged recovery, lasting consequences
  • Compensation for relatives of a deceased person – for the grief of the loss
  • Default interest from the moment provided by law

The amount of non-pecuniary damage is set on the basis of fairness and case law. Insurers often offer considerably less than what a court would award.

Procedure – insurer first, then the court

  1. Filing a written claim with the insurer with all documents – accident report, medical records, proof of expenses.
  2. The insurer must decide within the statutory period (up to three months from the claim).
  3. If it refuses, does not respond or offers an insufficient sum – a lawsuit against the insurer.

The claim is brought directly against the insurer – you do not need to sue the at-fault driver. The limitation period is five years, but it is best to gather evidence immediately.

When there is a criminal case

If there are injured persons, criminal proceedings are usually brought against the at-fault driver. As a lawyer for victims of crime I can represent you there too. If you are the one charged – see criminal cases and drink- and drug-driving.

Frequently asked questions

Must I accept the insurer's offer?

No. You can refuse and bring a claim. If you accept the amount as final, it will later be difficult to claim the difference.

How much does a lawyer for accident compensation cost?

Various arrangements are possible, including a success-based fee. In court, the insurer pays the costs if it loses.

Is a passenger in the at-fault car entitled to compensation?

As a rule, the passenger can claim compensation from the at-fault driver's third-party liability insurer. The specific statutory exceptions are checked in each case.
