Debt Recovery in Sofia

Someone owes you money – unpaid invoices, a loan, rent or compensation? The law provides fast-track procedures for debt recovery.

From the unpaid amount to money in your account

  1. Analysis of the claim – is there written evidence, when did it fall due, is limitation running.
  2. Demand letter – often enough, especially when sent by a lawyer.
  3. Order-for-payment procedure – a fast and inexpensive procedure before the district court.
  4. Statement of claim – when the debtor objects or the claim is disputed.
  5. Enforcement – garnishment of accounts and salaries, attachment of property through a bailiff.

Enforcement order – the fastest route

For monetary claims an application for an enforcement order can be filed with the district court at the debtor's address. The state fee is low and the court rules without summoning the parties. The debtor has two weeks to object. If no objection is filed, the order becomes final and a writ of execution is issued.

On the basis of certain documents – for example a promissory note or a notarial deed – immediate enforcement can also be requested, allowing enforcement even before the order becomes final.

If the debtor objects, the creditor has one month to bring a declaratory claim. Missing the deadline results in the order being set aside.

Limitation – do not wait

Type of claimLimitation period
Most claims under contracts and loans5 years
Rent, interest and other periodic payments3 years
Compensation and penalties for breach of contract3 years
Salaries3 years – see unpaid wages

Limitation is interrupted by acknowledgement of the debt, by bringing a claim or an application in the order-for-payment procedure, and by enforcement actions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I recover a loan made without a contract?

Yes, if you can prove that the money was handed over – by bank transfer, receipt, messages or witnesses (subject to certain limits on witness evidence).

What should I do if I receive an enforcement order?

If you do not owe the amount or part of it, file an objection within two weeks. It need not be reasoned, but it is best to seek advice.

Who pays the collection costs?

If successful, the court orders the debtor to pay the state fees and lawyer's fees.
