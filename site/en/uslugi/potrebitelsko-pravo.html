Unfair contract terms

Any term to the detriment of the consumer that does not meet the requirement of good faith and leads to a significant imbalance between the rights and obligations of the parties is unfair. Such terms are void unless individually negotiated.

  • The trader's right to change the price or terms unilaterally
  • Excessively high penalties and fees
  • Exclusion of the trader's liability
  • Terms the consumer had no real opportunity to read
  • Currency clauses that shift the entire risk to the consumer

Protecting traders

Traders also need protection – during Consumer Protection Commission inspections, penalty decrees, coercive administrative measures and disputes with customers. I help with:

  • Drafting terms and conditions, return and complaint policies that comply with the law
  • Responses to complaints and statements to the Commission
  • Appeals against penalty decrees of the Commission and other regulators
  • Appeals against orders for coercive administrative measures
  • Disputes with consumers before the courts and conciliation commissions

The path of a consumer complaint

  1. A written complaint or claim to the trader – keep a copy and proof of submission.
  2. A report to the Commission for Consumer Protection or a conciliation commission if the trader refuses.
  3. A claim before the district court – for a refund, compensation or a declaration that a term is void.

The second step is often enough, but when the trader does not cooperate, the next step is the court. Consumer cases are also part of my civil law practice.

Frequently asked questions

How long do I have to return goods bought online?

As a rule 14 days from receipt, without giving a reason. There are exceptions – for example made-to-order or perishable goods.

The trader rejects my complaint. What should I do?

File a written objection and, if refused, report to the Commission or bring a claim in court. I will review the documents and point out the fastest route.

Can I stop paying a loan if the contract is invalid?

If a consumer credit contract is invalid, you owe only the net amount received, without interest and fees. This is established by the court.

Do you also defend companies against the Commission?

Yes. My experience at the Commission allows me to defend traders effectively both during inspections and in court.
