Civil cases – the most common reason to need a lawyer

Civil law governs relations between people and companies – contracts, property, obligations, compensation. Most people need a civil lawyer when someone does not pay them, when they have signed a contract that is not being performed, or when they have suffered damage and want compensation.

My work does not necessarily start with a lawsuit. Often the most effective solution is a well-written demand letter, negotiations or a settlement. But when it comes to court, you will be prepared – with clear evidence and a realistic assessment of your chances.

How a civil case proceeds

  1. Consultation and documents. I review the contracts, correspondence, invoices and evidence.
  2. Demand letter / attempt at out-of-court settlement. Many disputes end here.
  3. Statement of claim or application for an enforcement order. I choose the fastest and cheapest route.
  4. Exchange of pleadings. The defendant has a deadline to respond, and the claimant – to reply.
  5. Open hearing. Witnesses, expert reports, closing arguments.
  6. Judgment and appeal. Appeal and, where there are grounds, cassation.
  7. Enforcement. Writ of execution and assistance before a bailiff.

Civil cases in Sofia are heard by the Sofia District Court at 54 Tsar Boris III Blvd. and by the Sofia City Court in the Palace of Justice – depending on the value of the claim and the type of dispute.

Contract law – drafting contracts

I draft and review contracts for:

  • sale and purchase, lease and loan
  • leasing, contract for work and services
  • assignment of receivables and promissory notes
  • commission, freight forwarding and carriage contracts

I defend clients in and out of court when a contract is not performed, draft wills and gift contracts, and assist in concluding settlements between disputing parties.

The court does not apply limitation of its own motion – but if the debtor invokes it, the claim becomes unenforceable in court. So do not delay bringing your claim.

Consumers and traders

Some civil disputes are consumer disputes – defective goods, refused warranty repairs, loans with unfair terms. Special rules of the Consumer Protection Act and the Consumer Credit Act apply – see consumer law.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a civil case cost?

The costs include the state fee (usually 4% of the value of the claim), the lawyer's fee and possibly expert reports. If you win, the court orders the losing party to pay the costs.

How long does a civil case in Sofia take?

Usually from a few months to a year at first instance, depending on complexity and the court's workload.

Must every dispute go to court?

No. Many disputes are resolved with a demand letter, negotiations or mediation. Court is the last step, not the first.

Can you represent me if I live outside Sofia or abroad?

Yes. Documents can be prepared remotely, and court representation is carried out under a power of attorney.
