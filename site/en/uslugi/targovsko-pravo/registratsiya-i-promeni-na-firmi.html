Which legal form to choose

FormSuitable forLiability
ET (sole trader)Independent activity with a small turnoverUnlimited – with all personal assets
EOOD (sole-owner Ltd)A single owner who wants limited liabilityLimited to the company's assets
OOD (Ltd)Two or more partnersLimited to the company's assets
AD / EAD (joint-stock company)Larger projects, attracting investorsLimited; more complex management

For most entrepreneurs an EOOD or OOD is a suitable choice – limited liability, flexible management and low costs.

How registration works

  1. Checking and reserving the company name.
  2. Drafting the articles of association, the decision appointing the manager and declarations.
  3. Paying in the capital to a capital-raising account.
  4. Filing the application with the Commercial Register at the Registry Agency – on paper or electronically.
  5. Registering the beneficial owners.
  6. VAT and other registrations, if required.

Corporate changes

Every change in circumstances must be filed with the Commercial Register. The most common changes I assist with:

  • Change of manager or adding a second manager
  • Change of registered office and management address
  • Change of the scope of activity
  • Transfer of company shares – by a contract with notarisation of the signatures and content
  • Admission and exclusion of partners
  • Capital increase or reduction
  • Conversion of a sole trader into an EOOD
  • Liquidation of a company

Branches, transformation and NGOs

Branch of a foreign trader

Foreign companies can do business in Bulgaria through a branch entered in the Commercial Register. A branch is not a separate legal entity, but it has a manager and keeps separate accounts.

Company transformation

Transformation – merger by acquisition, merger, spin-off, division or change of legal form – requires a transformation agreement or plan, an auditor's review in certain cases, and protection of creditors. The procedure is complex and the deadlines must be strictly observed.

NGO registration

Associations and foundations are registered in the Register of Non-Profit Legal Entities at the Registry Agency. I draft the statutes, founding minutes and applications, and for public-benefit activities – the documents for registration in that status.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to go to the Registry Agency in person?

No. With a power of attorney or an electronic signature the lawyer files all documents on your behalf.

How are shares in an OOD transferred?

By a written contract with notarisation of the signatures and content, after a resolution of the general meeting admitting the new partner, and entry in the Commercial Register.

Can I register a company if I am a foreigner?

Yes. Foreign nationals and companies can incorporate companies in Bulgaria, provided the requirements for the documents and their translation are met.
