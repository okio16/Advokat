When you need a criminal lawyer

Criminal proceedings rarely start “on schedule”. Usually it is a phone call summoning you to the police, a relative arrested during the night or a letter from the prosecutor's office. At that moment the most important thing is not to make statements, not to sign documents you do not understand and to contact a lawyer as early as possible. Decisions taken in the first 24–72 hours often determine the course of the whole case.

As a criminal defence lawyer in Sofia I defend people charged as accused persons, defendants at trial, witnesses who are concerned about their position, and victims who want to be heard and compensated.

  • Summons for questioning at a police station or by an investigator
  • Arrest by the police or detention by a prosecutor and a request for a remand measure
  • Being formally charged and the presentation of the investigation file
  • An indictment filed with the court or an offer for a plea agreement
  • Personal search, search of premises and seizure at home, in an office or a car
  • Victims of crime who want compensation and an active role in the proceedings

How criminal proceedings work in Bulgaria

Bulgarian criminal procedure has two main phases – pre-trial and trial. Understanding them helps you know what lies ahead and what can be done at each stage.

PhaseWho conducts the caseWhat the defence does
Preliminary check / pre-trial proceedingsPolice investigator or investigating magistrate supervised by a prosecutorAttends interviews, requests evidence, appeals unlawful actions, prepares the position
Remand measureDistrict or regional (city) court at the prosecutor's requestOpposes custody, proposes a lighter measure, appeals within 3 days
Trial – first instanceDistrict or regional court depending on the gravity of the offencePlea agreement, summary trial or full trial, closing speech
Appeal and cassationCity / appellate court, Supreme Court of CassationAppeals against the judgment, new evidence, legal arguments

At each stage there are procedural deadlines that cannot be restored if missed. That is why the defence lawyer should be involved from the first police summons, not only when the case reaches court.

Cases I handle

Offences against property

Theft and robbery, embezzlement, fraud, handling stolen goods. Here there is often room for a “minor case” qualification, compensation of the damage and a lighter sentence.

Drug offences

Possession for personal use, possession with intent to supply, cultivation. The quantity, the type of substance and the lawfulness of the search are decisive – more on the drug cases page.

Traffic offences

Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs, driving without a licence, road accidents with injured persons. Apart from the sentence, the fate of the car and the driving licence matters too.

Offences against the person

Bodily harm, threats, hooliganism, privately prosecuted offences (insult and defamation), including defence in sexual offence cases, where discretion is paramount.

Corporate offences

Tax offences, document fraud, breach of trust, offences by officials. Defending managers and employees requires a combination of criminal and commercial law.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to give a statement to the police without a lawyer?

No. The accused has the right to remain silent and to ask for a defence lawyer. It is best to discuss the situation with a lawyer first and only then decide whether and what to say.

How long can I be detained?

Police detention lasts up to 24 hours. A prosecutor may order detention of up to 72 hours in order to bring you before the court that decides on the remand measure.

What is a plea agreement and is it worth it?

A plea agreement is an arrangement between the prosecutor and the defence on the sentence, approved by the court. It can be beneficial, but it has the effect of a final judgment, so the decision should be taken after assessing the evidence.

Can I be released from criminal liability?

For some less serious intentional offences and for negligent offences the law allows release from criminal liability with an administrative penalty – a fine. The conditions are strict and are assessed case by case.

Do you work outside office hours in case of arrest?

In case of arrest I respond as quickly as possible, including outside regular office hours. Call the office phone.

How is the fee for a criminal case determined?

According to the complexity of the case, the stage of the proceedings and the number of hearings, in line with Ordinance No. 1 on lawyers' fees. The fee is agreed in writing in advance.
