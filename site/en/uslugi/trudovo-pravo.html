Employment disputes have short deadlines

Most employment disputes start unexpectedly – with a dismissal order, an unpaid salary or an accident at work. The Bulgarian Labour Code gives employees strong protection, but it must be sought in time. A claim to annul a dismissal, for example, must be filed within two months – after that even the most unlawful dismissal stands.

Employees do not pay court fees in employment cases when they are claimants. This makes protection accessible to everyone.

Civil servants

Civil servants are not employed under the Labour Code but under the Civil Servants Act. Their disputes – about appointment, appraisal, disciplinary penalties and dismissal – are decided by the administrative court, and the deadlines are even shorter: 14 days from notification of the act. Here my experience in the administration and in administrative cases is particularly useful.

Workplace accidents and death at work

In the event of a workplace accident – and in case of death, to the relatives of the deceased – the employer owes compensation for pecuniary and non-pecuniary damage. I help with declaring the accident, collecting evidence and filing the claim – more on the unpaid wages and workplace accidents page.

Frequently asked questions

Should I sign the dismissal order?

Your signature only confirms that you received it, not that you agree. Note the date of receipt. If you refuse, the employer may certify the refusal with witnesses.

Is there a court fee in an employment case?

No, when the claimant is an employee. The fees are imposed on the employer if it loses the case.

Can I be dismissed while on sick leave?

As a rule, the employer may dismiss an employee on leave, including sick leave, only with prior permission from the Labour Inspectorate on certain grounds. Breaching this rule makes the dismissal unlawful.

Do you also act for employers?

Yes. I help employers comply with the procedures and represent them in employment cases and inspections.
