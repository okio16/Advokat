The office and how to get there

The Palace of Justice at 2 Vitosha Blvd. houses the Sofia City Court, the Sofia Court of Appeal and the Supreme Court of Cassation, and the Administrative Court Sofia City is at 17 Georg Washington St. Meetings with clients from the centre can take place at the office, before a court hearing or online.

The address is 7 Strazhitsa St., floor 2, office 10, Nadezhda 2. From the centre the office is reached directly by metro line M2, without changing.

Please call +359 88 399 6666 in advance to arrange a convenient time.

How I can help

I handle criminal, administrative, civil, family, inheritance, employment and commercial cases – from the first consultation to the court's decision. All services are described on the Legal services page.

The fee is agreed in writing in advance, in line with Ordinance No. 1 on lawyers' fees.

All of Sofia and the whole country

I work with clients from every district of Sofia and from all over Bulgaria. If you cannot come to the office, the consultation can take place by phone or online, and documents can be sent by email. See also all areas.

Legal services for clients from the area

Criminal law

Legal defence before the police, investigators, prosecutors and courts for people accused of crimes and for victims of crime.

Coercive measures and detention

Signature bond, bail, house arrest, custody, restraining orders, suspension from office, travel ban.

Administrative and administrative penalty law

Legal protection before state, municipal and judicial authorities. Challenging administrative acts and appealing penalty decrees.

State Liability Act claims

Compensation for unlawful charges, detention, unlawful administrative acts and slow justice.

Commercial law and companies

Registration of legal entities and changes in their circumstances, commercial contracts, advice and litigation.

Contract disputes and termination of contracts

Non-performance, termination of contracts, penalties, drafting and reviewing contracts.
